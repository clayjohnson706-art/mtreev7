Privacy Policy — mTree

Last updated: July 2026

mTree ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and profile picture, provided via Google Sign-In when you create an account.
  • Profile details you choose to add: gender, date of birth, country, and your selected Deity. Country is used only to detect your local currency for pricing and donation amounts.
  • Manifestation data: goals, sacrifices, streaks, ritual history, and any notes or testimonies you write.
  • Donation & subscription status: if you choose to donate or subscribe to Premium, we store the donation amount, currency, and your Premium/subscription status — never your card or payment details (see Section 3).
  • Usage data: such as app interactions needed to operate core features (e.g. daily streaks, reminder preferences).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to operate the app: authenticate you, save your manifestation progress, personalize your experience (deity, affirmations, currency), and — if you opt in — display your completed manifestations on the public Community Wall.

3. Payments & Donations

Premium subscription payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Voluntary donations made within the app are also processed through Google Play Billing where applicable.

mTree never receives or stores your card number, bank details, or any raw payment credentials — Google handles all payment processing. We only receive and store the resulting donation amount, currency, and your subscription/Premium status to keep your account in sync.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. We share data only with: Google (for Sign-In authentication and Play Billing payment processing), and our cloud database/hosting providers who process data on our behalf to run the app.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a secured MongoDB database. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed immediately and permanently, except for anonymized transaction records we may be legally required to retain for tax/accounting purposes (see our Account & Data Deletion page for details).

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. You can exercise most of these rights directly in the app (Settings), or by contacting us using the details below.

8. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time directly in the app: open Settings → scroll to the bottom → "Delete Account" → confirm. This immediately and permanently removes your profile, manifestations, garden, and saved items. If you are unable to access the app, you may request deletion by emailing us (see Contact below) from the email address associated with your account. Full details are on our Account & Data Deletion page.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored on servers located outside your country of residence. By using mTree, you consent to this transfer and processing as described in this policy.

10. Children's Privacy

mTree is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, contact us at: nextleveldev@gmail.com
