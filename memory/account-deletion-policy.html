Account & Data Deletion — mTree

Last updated: February 2026

You can permanently delete your mTree account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how, in line with Google Play's Data Safety requirements.

Option 1 — Delete In-App (Instant)

RECOMMENDED
  1. Open the mTree app and sign in
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Your account and data are removed immediately and permanently — this cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request Deletion by Email

If you no longer have access to the app or your account, you can request deletion by emailing us from the email address associated with your mTree account:

nextleveldev@gmail.com

Please include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line. We will verify your identity and process the request within 7 business days.
