Refund Policy — mTree

Last updated: July 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refunds are handled for purchases made within the mTree app ("we", "our", "the app"). It covers two types of payments: Premium Subscriptions (First Month, Monthly, 6-Month, and Yearly plans) and voluntary Donations.

1. Premium Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are billed and processed entirely through Google Play Billing. mTree does not directly collect or store your payment details, and does not directly process refunds.

  • All subscription charges are subject to Google Play's own refund policies and timelines.
  • You can request a refund directly through Google Play within the eligible refund window (typically up to 48 hours for a new purchase, or per Google's current policy at the time of purchase).
  • You can cancel auto-renewal at any time from Google Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future billing, but you keep Premium access until the end of the current billing period — no partial refund is issued for the remaining days.
  • Switching between plans (e.g. Monthly to Yearly) takes effect per Google Play's proration rules; any prorated amount is calculated and refunded/charged by Google Play, not by mTree directly.

2. How to Request a Subscription Refund

Since payments are processed by Google Play, please request refunds directly through Google:

  • Open the Google Play Store app → Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history
  • Find the mTree charge → tap "Request a refund" or "Report a problem"
  • Alternatively, visit Google Play Help — Refunds

If Google Play declines your refund request and you believe there was a billing error (e.g. duplicate charge, service not delivered), contact us at the email below and we will assist where possible.

3. Donations

Donations made within the app (e.g. after completing a manifestation) are voluntary contributions and are, as a general rule, non-refundable, since they are not payments for a product or service.

Exceptions are made in good faith for genuine errors, such as:

  • An accidental or duplicate donation charge
  • A technical error resulting in an incorrect amount being charged

If any of the above applies to you, contact us within 7 days of the transaction with your account email and transaction details, and we will review your request.

4. Free Trials

mTree does not currently offer a free trial on any subscription plan — you are charged at the time of purchase. If a free trial is introduced in the future, its terms (including cancellation deadlines and how billing begins) will be clearly shown before you confirm the purchase, and this policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Contact Us

For any refund-related questions or billing errors not resolved through Google Play, email us at: nextleveldev@gmail.com
